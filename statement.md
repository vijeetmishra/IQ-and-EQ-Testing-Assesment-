# Problem Statement: Comprehensive IQ & EQ Assessment Suite

## 1. Overview
The objective of this project is to design, develop, and deploy a robust Multiple-Choice Questionnaire (MCQ) assessment framework capable of evaluating both the **Intelligence Quotient (IQ)** and **Emotional Quotient (EQ)** of a candidate. While traditional assessments isolate cognitive reasoning or emotional awareness, this tool aims to provide a holistic profile by integrating logical-mathematical puzzles with situational emotional dilemmas.

---

## 2. Objectives & Scope
- **Dual-Metric Evaluation:** Measure logical deduction, pattern recognition, and spatial reasoning (IQ) alongside empathy, self-awareness, emotional regulation, and social skills (EQ).
- **MCQ Format:** Formulate clear, concise multiple-choice questions with one correct answer for IQ modules, and nuanced, behavior-based multiple-choice options (graded on a Likert or weighted scale) for EQ modules.
- **Actionable Insights:** Generate a comprehensive post-assessment report highlighting cognitive strengths, emotional maturity, and areas for professional or personal development.

---

## 3. Core Requirements

### A. IQ Assessment Module (Cognitive Intelligence)
The IQ section must test logical, numerical, and verbal capabilities without cultural or linguistic bias.
- **Pattern Recognition:** Sequences involving numbers, shapes, or abstract matrices.
- **Logical Reasoning:** Syllogisms, deductive/inductive reasoning puzzles, and chronological ordering.
- **Quantitative Aptitude:** Word problems testing applied mathematics, probability, and spatial manipulation.

### B. EQ Assessment Module (Emotional Intelligence)
The EQ section must present realistic, scenario-based dilemmas that assess how a candidate perceives, integrates, understands, and manages emotions.
- **Self-Awareness & Regulation:** Recognizing personal emotional triggers and maintaining composure under high-pressure scenarios.
- **Empathy & Social Awareness:** Interpreting verbal and non-verbal cues from others in collaborative or conflict-driven environments.
- **Relationship Management:** Conflict resolution, leadership style, and motivational aptitude in team settings.

---

## 4. Evaluation Rubric

| Criterion | Description | Weight | Target Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Logical Accuracy (IQ)** | Precision in solving pattern, math, and deductive reasoning items. | 50% | High accuracy under timed constraints. |
| **Emotional Resonance (EQ)** | Alignment of chosen responses with emotionally intelligent, constructive outcomes. | 40% | High empathy, collaboration, and stress-management indicators. |
| **Consistency & Reliability** | Internal coherence across similar distractor patterns and test-retest stability. | 10% | Low variance in reliability indices. |

---

## 5. Deliverables
1. A question bank containing at least 20 validated MCQs (10 IQ, 10 EQ).
2. Scoring logic documentation explaining how weighted EQ choices and binary IQ choices are aggregated into a final dual-score profile.
3. A sample candidate report template.
